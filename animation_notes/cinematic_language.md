# Cinematic Language - Leela.exe

## Purpose
These notes translate manga page design into animation-minded staging logic so scenes feel directed rather than randomly illustrated.

## Scene Rhythm
Leela.exe should feel like it has breath. Even static images should imply tempo.

Preferred rhythm:
1. hold on atmosphere
2. narrow into character feeling
3. land on revelation or decision

## Camera Philosophy
### For Divine Child Scenes
- use intimate framing
- let eye lines and hand placement carry playfulness
- combine warmth with cosmic scale hints

### For Manu Scenes
- begin grounded and observational
- frame him in relation to water and horizon
- let the world feel larger than him without making him weak

### For Matsya Scenes
- start with intimacy
- escalate scale through composition, not only narration
- use water movement to lead the camera eye

## Page-Turn Logic
Strong page turns should end on:
- a new scale reveal
- a prophetic line
- a changed emotional truth
- a visual threshold such as storm arrival, impossible growth, or divine eye contact

Final pages should end on:
- fulfilled intervention
- emotional release after pressure
- a new-cycle image
- divine withdrawal or completion, if appropriate

## Transition Devices
Use these recurring transitions between beats:
- water ripple becoming the next panel boundary
- reflection leading into a new point of view
- sky darkening across a sequence
- hand-to-object transitions such as bowl, vessel, seed, rope, or scripture

## Silence
Silence is important in this project.

Best uses:
- before a revelation
- after an instruction from Matsya
- when the environment itself is speaking through omen
- when a childlike scene tips into cosmic responsibility

## Sound-Implied Visuals
Even in still-image prompt work, compositions can imply sound:
- distant thunder through diagonal lightning and crowd posture
- quiet river through soft reflections and minimal motion
- divine arrival through reduced clutter and concentrated glow
- catastrophe through debris direction and broken horizon lines

## Acting Notes
### Vishnu
- playful concentration
- quick transition into responsibility
- never frantic

### Manu
- dignity under confusion
- protective instinct before comprehension
- resolve growing through stillness, not shouting

### Matsya
- calm certainty
- protective command
- scale without panic

## Animation-Minded Continuity Rule
Treat each page like a keyframe from a larger sequence. Ask:
- what changed since the last image?
- what must carry forward?
- where should the viewer's eye land first?
- what is the held note at the end of the page?
