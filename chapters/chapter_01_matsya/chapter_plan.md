# Chapter 1 Detailed Plan - Matsya Avatar

## Guiding Principle
Page 1 already functions as a prologue. Pages 2 through 10 must now deliver the full Matsya storyline from omen to preservation to closure.

## Best Structural Choice
Recommended path: enter the mythic timeline on page 2 and stay there long enough for Manu, water, and omen to become emotionally real.

Reason:
- page 1 already delivered the simulation concept clearly
- returning to Vishnu too soon would dilute the mythic immersion
- Matsya works best when the human-scale mystery is allowed to build
- the remaining nine pages need to spend their space on a complete avatar arc

## Page 2 Recommendation
Page 2 should be slower and cleaner than page 1.

Suggested page function:
- establish Manu in ritual relation to water
- imply the world is slightly off before anything impossible happens
- end with the first subtle sign that the river is responding

## Recurring Emotional Arc For The Chapter
1. wonder
2. care
3. unease
4. awe
5. duty
6. survival
7. relief
8. closure

## Visual Escalation Ladder
### Phase 1 - Quiet Omen
- ripples
- reflections
- unusual stillness
- sacred morning light

### Phase 2 - Impossible Growth
- container mismatch
- water intelligence
- scale changes
- denser shadows and clouds

### Phase 3 - Prophetic Authority
- Matsya eye contact feels deliberate
- speech or command becomes explicit
- negative space increases around the avatar

### Phase 4 - Flood Threshold
- skies darken
- distance scale increases
- human crowds, animals, or tools begin to matter

### Phase 5 - Preserved Continuity
- storm gives way to sacred stillness
- survival is legible on the page
- Matsya's purpose feels fulfilled
- closing image implies transition, not unfinished business

## Chapter 1 Dialogue Guidance
- keep dialogue sparse after page 1
- let Manu speak plainly
- let Matsya speak with weight and brevity
- use captions only when needed to bridge mythic time or scale

## Continuity Traps To Avoid
1. Do not repeat page 1's full exposition mode on page 2.
2. Do not show maximum-scale Matsya too early in-sequence.
3. Do not let the simulation metaphor erase ritual sincerity.
4. Do not rush Manu from calm to panic without intermediate beats.
5. Do not leave the flood-preservation mission unresolved by page 10.
