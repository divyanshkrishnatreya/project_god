# Dharma Engine - Leela.exe

## Purpose
This file defines how balance works across the world. It is the logic layer beneath plot escalation.

## Core Statement
Dharma is the simulation's stability condition. It is not a single law, king, religion, or moral slogan. It is the living coherence that allows beings, societies, and nature to remain aligned enough for meaningful life to continue.

When Dharma weakens, reality starts to feel strained before it fully breaks.

## Dharma Inputs
The world trends toward stability when the following are upheld:
- truthfulness
- rightful duty
- compassion
- reciprocity
- stewardship of life
- reverence for the sacred
- restraint in the use of power

The world trends toward instability when the following spread:
- greed detached from responsibility
- arrogance against cosmic order
- violence for dominance
- hoarding of life or knowledge
- desecration of sacred trust
- rule manipulation that protects corruption

## Karma As Balancing Algorithm
Karma is the corrective balancing process that redistributes consequence across time, relationship, memory, and rebirth.

Important interpretation rules:
1. Karma is not petty revenge.
2. Karma may act slowly.
3. Innocent people can still suffer inside collective decline.
4. Karma can arrive as insight, loss, reversal, delay, blessing, burden, or rebirth conditions.
5. Characters should not treat Karma like a visible scoreboard.

## Dharma Instability Symptoms
As Dharma drops, the world may show:
- strange weather patterns
- disturbed animal behavior
- prophetic dreams
- fragmented memory or deja vu
- reflections that move incorrectly
- sacred chants causing visible ripples in air or water
- cycles of authority failing in quick succession
- water becoming the first medium of warning

## Instability Scale
| Level | State | Story Symptoms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Stable | Nature, ritual, and social life feel coherent |
| 1 | Friction | Omens, unease, and local disruptions begin |
| 2 | Fracture | Repeated signs, failed institutions, weather anomalies |
| 3 | Critical | Prophecy, mass danger, sacred intervention likely |
| 4 | Collapse | Cycle-ending crisis, flood, war, or civilizational break |

## Yuga Cycle Logic
Yugas are seasonal resets of the civilizational simulation. They do not fully erase the past. Instead, they preserve residue through myth, instinct, and sacred memory.

| Yuga | Stability Profile | Narrative Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Satya | Highest Dharma coherence | Luminous, spacious, truthful |
| Treta | Reduced harmony | Noble but strained |
| Dvapara | Fragmenting values | Complex, political, emotionally split |
| Kali | Lowest coherence | Dense, accelerated, corruptible |

Transition rules:
1. A Yuga shift should feel like climate change for reality itself.
2. Not every chapter needs a Yuga transition.
3. Echoes from prior cycles may appear as myths, instincts, symbols, or inexplicable familiarity.

## Shiva Reset Clause
Shiva holds the sacred potential for total system reset. This is not ordinary destruction. It is transformational dissolution when the current state can no longer sustain meaningful continuation.

Usage guardrails:
1. Shiva's presence should radically change the atmosphere.
2. Reset logic belongs to extreme endgame stakes, not routine correction.
3. When Shiva is invoked, the story should feel cosmic, solemn, and irreversible.

## Sages As Diagnostic Nodes
Sages, ascetics, seers, and deeply disciplined minds can detect Dharma instability sooner than rulers or armies.

Their observations often come through:
- meditation
- dreams
- mantra resonance
- symbolic visions
- silence where sound should be

## Chapter 1 Application - Matsya
For the Matsya arc, Dharma instability should first be sensed through water, scale shifts, and prophetic unease rather than open catastrophe. The chapter should move from subtle anomaly to undeniable warning.

## Writing Guardrails
1. Do not moralize every event in simplistic terms.
2. Keep Dharma relational and ecological, not merely legal.
3. Let the environment reveal instability before exposition does.
