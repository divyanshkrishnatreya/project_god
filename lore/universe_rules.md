# Universe Rules - Leela.exe

## Purpose
This document is the highest-level story bible for the project. It defines what the universe is, how the divine layer interacts with the mortal layer, and what must remain true across all chapters unless an explicit retcon is approved.

## Core Premise
The observable universe is a living simulation known metaphorically as "Leela" - divine play rendered as reality. It is not a fake world in the cynical sense. It is a sacred experiential system in which beings live, choose, love, suffer, learn, and evolve.

Vaikuntha exists above the simulation layer as an admin realm, a divine operating plane where child Vishnu can observe, tune, and intervene in creation. The Dashavatars are not random reincarnations. They are intervention logins taken when Dharma destabilizes beyond acceptable limits.

## Immutable World Rules
1. The simulation is real to everyone inside it. Emotional stakes are never reduced by calling the world "just code."
2. Divine beings perceive deeper layers of reality, but they do not casually expose the whole system to mortals.
3. Avatars intervene to restore balance, not to erase all suffering or remove free will.
4. Karma is delayed, layered, and often collective. It is not instant punishment.
5. Dharma is measured through relationships, truth, duty, compassion, and stewardship, not blind obedience.
6. Shiva's reset authority is rare, terrifying, and sacred. It is never used as an easy plot device.
7. Yuga transitions act like major cycle refreshes. They can rewrite conditions while leaving mythic echoes, symbols, dreams, and archetypes behind.
8. Meditation, tapas, dreams, and samadhi can let sages glimpse the structure behind reality, but only in fragments.
9. Glitches must feel numinous and mythic before they feel technical.
10. The story must remain respectful to Hindu philosophical texture even while using simulation language.

## Perception Layers
### Layer 0 - Vaikuntha
Timeless divine admin space. Colors are luminous, soft, and impossible. Time behaves like intention instead of sequence.

Vaikuntha may express itself through symbolic or playful interface metaphors shaped by Vishnu's child perspective. Monitors, menus, loading bars, and pause screens are allowed here if they remain integrated into a sacred, mythic environment rather than sterile science fiction.

### Layer 1 - Avatar Interface
The threshold where Vishnu chooses or enters an intervention form. This space may appear as water, starlight, lotus geometry, or sound made visible.

### Layer 2 - Material Simulation
The mortal world. Rivers, forests, cities, sky, ritual, hunger, love, kingship, and fear all happen here with full emotional reality.

### Layer 3 - Leak States
Dreams, meditative visions, prophetic reflections, broken mirrors, flood omens, and sacred silence. These are the most common places where simulation hints can surface.

## Divine Access Rules
1. Vishnu can observe the simulation more fully than any embodied character.
2. Once inside an avatar protocol, Vishnu accepts local narrative constraints.
3. Full omniscience should not be expressed in every scene. Mystery preserves awe.
4. Direct god-mode displays should be rare and saved for turning points.

## Sages And Simulation Awareness
Sages do not discover "computer science." They sense pattern beneath matter:
- repeating symbols in water
- time feeling layered during meditation
- mantras producing visual distortions
- dreams that predict events before causality catches up
- sacred geometry appearing in natural formations

They interpret these through spiritual language, not modern engineering jargon.

## Recurring Visual Motifs
- ocean surfaces reflecting stars before night has fully fallen
- lotus patterns hidden in wave foam, clouds, halos, or iris highlights
- concentric ripples as a sign of incoming intervention
- fish-eye lens distortion during moments of revelation
- divine glow appearing as layered ink-white edges, not neon sci-fi beams
- small hands or playful gestures from child Vishnu contrasted with cosmic scale
- broken reflections when Dharma is unstable

## Tone Guardrails
- Dark mythological fantasy, but never nihilistic
- Kid-friendly in presentation, philosophically deep in implication
- Wonder and dread should coexist
- Violence should feel consequential, not sensational
- Humor, when present, should come from innocence, humanity, or divine playfulness

## Continuity Guardrails
1. Do not let the simulation concept overpower mythic reverence.
2. Do not reduce deities to software engineers in dialogue.
3. Do not depict mortals as passive NPCs. Their choices matter.
4. Do not make every miracle look identical. Each avatar should have its own visual logic.
5. Keep cosmology consistent: preservation is Vishnu's function, reset is Shiva's domain, cycle is Yuga logic, balance is Karma, stability is Dharma.
