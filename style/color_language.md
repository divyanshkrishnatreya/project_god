# Color Language - Leela.exe

## Purpose
Even if later pages are adapted into monochrome manga variants, the project still needs a stable color script for current painted pages, references, and prompt continuity.

## Global Rule
Color should support spiritual clarity, mood, and continuity. It should never become a noisy fantasy palette that breaks the grounded mythic atmosphere.

## Established Page 1 Palette Signals
Page 1 already suggests the project's base palette:
- warm parchment captions and title card tones
- amber-gold interior lighting in Vaikuntha
- cool blue cosmic screen light
- storm-gray and steel-blue catastrophe imagery
- pale gold divine ornament accents

## Core Palette Families
### Divine Preservation
- deep indigo
- river blue
- moonlit silver
- pale gold

Use for Vishnu presence, sacred calm, guidance, and moments when order reasserts itself.

### Mortal Earth
- clay brown
- muted saffron
- weathered ivory
- dry leaf green

Use for Manu, ritual objects, simple garments, and human-scale environments.

### Dharma Instability
- storm gray
- green-black water
- ash blue
- ember-orange in restrained catastrophe scenes

Use for unease, prophetic weather, and system stress.

### Transition And Flood
- teal-black depths
- silver foam
- white glare
- horizon gold fading into storm

Use when the chapter pivots from omen to survival stakes.

## Character Color Anchors
### Vishnu
- blue skin in the indigo family
- warm gold ornament accents
- cool cosmic light can rim the face in admin scenes

### Matsya
- silver-blue scales
- teal depth tones
- gold crown or jewel accents in full divine reveal

### Manu
- earth neutrals
- muted warm cloth
- subtle sacred thread or ritual accent

## Monochrome Translation
When rendered in black and white:
1. Indigo translates to rich dark midtone or shaped black with clean highlight edge.
2. Gold translates to bright rimlight, warm value separation, or symbolic halo.
3. Water tones translate through reflective texture and value shifts, not flat gray fills.
4. Catastrophe scenes need layered blacks, white foam, and lightning contrast.

## Glow Rules
- divine glow should preserve form readability
- avoid oversaturating the whole frame
- glow is strongest on edges, reflections, eyes, jewelry highlights, and water surfaces
- use contrast between warm sacred highlights and cool environmental shadows

## Emotional Color Beats For Chapter 1
| Beat | Lighting Intent |
| --- | --- |
| Vaikuntha playfulness | warm amber interior plus cool screen light |
| First anomaly | soft silver-blue shimmer |
| Growing unease | warm tones swallowed by storm gray |
| Prophecy | stronger contrast, cold reflections, isolated highlights |
| Divine reveal | indigo, silver, and pale gold dominating the page |

## Palette Guardrails
1. Avoid bright neon accents outside intentional admin UI moments.
2. Avoid purple-heavy default fantasy grading.
3. Preserve atmospheric subtlety.
4. Reuse the same emotional color logic across chapters so readers feel continuity even when scenes change.
