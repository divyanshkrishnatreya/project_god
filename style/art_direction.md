# Art Direction - Leela.exe

## Core Style Statement
Leela.exe should look like a cinematic manga-webtoon hybrid with painterly detail, dark mythological fantasy atmosphere, and clear emotional acting. The world can hold both sacred antiquity and playful modern admin imagery, but the overall impression must still feel intentional, reverent, and unified.

## Primary Aesthetic Goals
- cinematic manga framing
- emotional closeups with expressive eyes
- highly detailed oceans, rivers, clouds, and skies
- strong ink contrast and shaped blacks
- dramatic lighting that feels sacred, not stagey
- atmospheric perspective for scale
- divine glow effects integrated into the rendering
- kid-friendly readability with philosophical depth

## Established Page 1 Style Signals
Page 1 locks in several important stylistic choices:
- full-color painterly rendering is allowed and currently canon-compatible
- black panel borders with caption boxes and speech balloons are part of the visual language
- Vaikuntha can contain ornate interiors plus modern simulation devices
- chapter title pages can be embedded directly into story layouts
- apocalyptic panels should feel dense, cinematic, and catastrophic without losing readability

## Rendering Approach
1. Use strong contour clarity even in painted scenes.
2. Reserve the densest detail for faces, jewelry, water edges, architecture, and divine moments.
3. Keep focal hierarchy clear so the eye is not lost in pure texture.

## Value Design
1. Prioritize readable silhouettes first.
2. Use deep blacks and heavy dark values for dread, storm, void, and catastrophe.
3. Use preserved highlights for divinity, water reflections, eyes, foam, and sacred aura edges.
4. Warm interiors should contrast with cold cosmic or flood imagery.

## Lighting Rules
- Dawn, dusk, candlelit interiors, and stormlight are preferred lighting families.
- Divine presence can create directional glow without flattening the scene.
- Admin-space scenes in Vaikuntha may use warm domestic light combined with cool cosmic screen light.
- Eye lights matter. A shift in eye light can mark a shift in revelation or duty.

## Environment Rendering
### Water
Water is a major character in the Matsya arc.
- render surface behavior carefully
- use reflections to foreshadow hidden truth
- keep wave rhythms expressive but believable
- show scale through ripple size, foam texture, and reflected sky distortion

### Sky
- skies should carry emotional weather
- use cloud forms to echo tension, awe, or transition
- highly detailed skies are encouraged, especially when linking divine and mortal layers

### Interiors
- Vaikuntha interiors can be domestic, ornate, and lived-in
- carved wood, framed iconography, soft daylight, and sacred objects are all compatible
- technology in these spaces should feel like a playful overlay, not sterile science fiction

## Camera Language
Use a filmic mix of:
- wide establishing shots for scale and atmosphere
- medium shots for relational clarity
- intense closeups for revelation and emotional beats
- occasional montage compression for mythic exposition

Avoid random angle changes that break spatial continuity.

## Character Rendering Priorities
1. Eyes and hands carry emotion.
2. Distinct silhouettes must survive even in detailed pages.
3. Divine characters should feel cleaner and more centered than mortal chaos.
4. Repeated costumes and hairstyles must stay consistent unless story logic changes them.

## Motion And Effects
- use motion lines sparingly and purposefully
- water movement can replace generic speed effects in many scenes
- divine glow should feel like pressure in the air, reflected in surfaces
- environmental particles can include mist, foam, ash-light, pollen-like sparks, or drifting star flecks

## Recurring Visual Motifs
- concentric ripples
- star reflections on water
- lotus or sacred geometry hidden in natural forms
- tiny figure versus enormous sky or wave
- childlike softness paired with cosmic scale
- interface text or menu framing only in explicit admin-space scenes

## Tone Balance
The tone should be accessible to younger readers at the surface level while rewarding older readers with symbolism, metaphysics, and emotional subtext.

## Style Drift Warnings
Do not let the project drift toward:
- glossy generic sci-fi UI dominating non-admin scenes
- generic superhero anatomy
- hypersexualized fantasy design
- comedy-chibi interruptions in serious scenes
- flat gradient-heavy art with no structure
- horror gore replacing sacred dread

## Page Approval Checklist
1. Is the focal point clear?
2. Are water and sky rendered with care?
3. Does the lighting reinforce the emotion?
4. Are divine elements integrated rather than pasted on?
5. Does the page still feel like the same manga as the previous one?
