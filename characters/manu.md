# Character Sheet - Manu

## Identity
Manu, also referenced in mythic language as Satyavrata or Vaivasvata Manu, is the mortal anchor of the Matsya arc. He is the witness, caretaker, and future preserver who must choose trust before proof becomes undeniable.

## Narrative Function
- human point of identification
- moral seriousness grounded in duty
- recipient of Matsya's warning
- custodian of continuity into the next cycle

## Apparent Age
Late 30s to early 50s. Mature enough to carry responsibility, active enough to move through crisis.

## Core Personality
- disciplined
- observant
- humble
- compassionate
- responsible
- initially rational before fully mystical

## Visual Canon
### Silhouette
Lean, grounded, practical. His stance should show a life shaped by ritual, kingship or stewardship, and responsibility.

### Face
- weathered but noble
- thoughtful eyes
- beard is canon-compatible based on the page 1 mythic recap image
- expressions should stay readable even in medium shot

### Hair
- dark, long, and tied or flowing in a controlled way
- should feel mythic but not ornamental

### Clothing
- simple dhoti or wrapped lower garment
- upper body may be bare or lightly draped in some scenes
- if royal identity is emphasized, keep accessories modest
- sacred thread or ritual marks are appropriate

### Props
- water vessel
- prayer offerings
- later chapter props may include seed bundles, scrolls, rope, and boat-preparation tools

## Emotional Arc For Chapter 1
1. Calm ritual focus or mythic reverence
2. Curiosity at the unusual fish
3. Protective tenderness
4. Growing unease as impossibility escalates
5. Awe mixed with duty when warning is received

## Behavior Rules
1. Manu acts before he fully understands.
2. His kindness is the reason the revelation deepens.
3. He should never feel foolish for helping the fish.
4. He is reverent without becoming passive.

## Continuity Anchors
1. Preserve grounded dignity.
2. Keep costume simple and repeatable.
3. If he gets wet, dirtied, exhausted, or injured, track it page to page.
4. His eyes are key to emotional continuity. They must clearly progress from curiosity to alarm to sacred resolve.

## Do Not
- make him a generic jeweled fantasy king
- over-ornament his design
- flatten him into a passive prophecy receiver
- let fear erase his dignity

## Prompt Tags
Manu, Satyavrata, grounded mythic human protagonist, ritual riverbank, compassionate protector, dark mythological fantasy, cinematic manga closeups
