# Character Sheet - Vishnu

## Identity
Child Vishnu is the hidden admin-player of the Leela simulation. He exists primarily in Vaikuntha and interfaces with the world through avatars, dreams, symbols, and rare direct scenes.

## Narrative Function
- keeper of continuity
- preserver of worlds
- playful cosmic intelligence
- emotional counterweight to dread
- bridge between innocence and absolute scale

## Apparent Age
Appears roughly 8 to 10 years old in divine-child form.

## Core Personality
- curious
- compassionate
- patient
- quietly mischievous
- sincere about duty even when playful
- never cruel

Vishnu should feel like a child without ever feeling small in wisdom.

## Visual Canon
### Silhouette
Small, graceful frame with poised stillness. Even seated, the body language should feel focused and centered.

### Face
- soft youthful cheeks
- large observant eyes
- warm, alert expression
- gaze can pivot from playful to ancient within a single beat

### Skin And Glow
- rich blue to deep indigo skin tone
- soft inner radiance, especially at the brow, hands, shoulders, and hair edge
- glow should feel sacred and painterly, not synthetic

### Hair
- dark black hair
- thick, curly, and slightly unruly in a childlike way
- often tied into a high bun or gathered style
- peacock feather accent is strongly associated with the established page 1 look

### Clothing And Ornaments
- warm golden or saffron-toned drape around the lower body
- divine child styling rather than adult kingly armor
- gold ornaments, earrings, and arm pieces are acceptable and already established visually
- jewelry should remain elegant and readable, not overly dense

### Established Page 1 Details
- seated at a desktop-style simulation workstation in Vaikuntha
- uses a monitor-like cosmic interface
- keyboard and modern UI metaphors are canon inside admin-space scenes
- ornate domestic-divine interior with framed art, carved furniture, and soft daylight

## Expression Range
| Emotion | Visual Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Playful focus | slight smile, intent eyes, active hands |
| Mild defiance | childlike seriousness, little frown, still respectful |
| Compassion | softened eyes, relaxed posture, warm rimlight |
| Cosmic authority | calm face, still body, wider aura, less clutter around him |

## Movement Language
- never clumsy
- seated scenes can still feel active through hands, shoulders, and eye line
- when using admin authority, interface reactions can ripple through screens, water, starlight, or lotus geometry
- transitions from play to responsibility should feel immediate and believable

## Speech Style
- short, clear lines
- affectionate and familiar in domestic scenes
- precise and calm in divine moments
- can sound like a child describing something with total certainty

## Role In Chapter 1
Page 1 establishes Vishnu directly in Vaikuntha before the Matsya mythic recap. This means the chapter opens with explicit simulation framing rather than hidden symbolism alone.

## Continuity Anchors
1. Preserve the youthful face, curly dark hair, and peacock feather accent.
2. Maintain blue skin and warm gold ornamentation.
3. In Vaikuntha scenes, keep the mix of domestic intimacy and cosmic scale.
4. His workstation setup should remain ornate, grounded, and integrated into the divine space.
5. When Vishnu is on-page, the composition should feel intentional and centered even if the setting is detailed.

## Do Not
- make him sarcastic
- make him hyperactive
- over-age him into a teen or adult between scenes
- strip away the domestic warmth established in page 1
