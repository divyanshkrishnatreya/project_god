# Character Sheet - Matsya

## Identity
Matsya is Vishnu's flood-preservation intervention protocol. In this arc, Matsya is the first avatar to guide Manu through an approaching cycle-breaking catastrophe.

## Narrative Function
- omen of incoming collapse
- guide across transition
- protector of continuity, seeds, and sacred memory
- first clear bridge between myth and simulation logic

## Core Presence
Matsya should feel protective first, uncanny second, terrifying only when cosmic scale becomes necessary.

## Established Page 1 Read
The first page already presents Matsya in mythic recap form as a giant sacred fish with regal/divine ornamentation, protecting a boat and confronting flood-scale chaos. Future pages should stay consistent with that sense of grandeur once full revelation is reached.

## Form Progression
### Stage 1 - Hand-Sized Fish
- small enough to be held or sheltered
- bright, alert eye
- unusual but beautiful scale sheen
- immediate emotional effect is curiosity

### Stage 2 - Vessel-Exceeding Fish
- visibly too large for pot, jar, or basin
- growth should feel miraculous, not grotesque
- surrounding water reacts with unusual stillness or halo ripples

### Stage 3 - River Guardian
- graceful, muscular aquatic body
- sacred ornament implied through natural form and selective adornment
- fins and tail can echo fabric, flame, or wave calligraphy

### Stage 4 - Divine Matsya
- enormous mythic fish form
- crown, crest, or jewel-bearing divine headpiece is now canon-compatible
- aura carries unmistakable Vishnu identity
- sea and sky visually reorganize around the presence

## Visual Canon
### Head And Eyes
- intelligent, compassionate eyes
- large, readable iris
- never blank or predatory
- eye highlights can contain star or lotus shapes during revelation moments

### Body
- sleek and elegant, not shark-like
- scales can shift between silver, moonlit blue, teal, and pale gold accents in color
- in monochrome, use reflective ink contrast and layered whites

### Fins And Tail
- lyrical and directional
- motion should guide the eye across the page
- avoid spiky monster silhouettes

### Aura
- water-bright halo
- ripple fields
- drifting specks like submerged stars
- storm water may part visually around the body

## Emotional Range
| Beat | Presence |
| --- | --- |
| First encounter | curious, gentle, luminous |
| Growth reveal | wondrous, slightly unsettling |
| Prophecy | solemn, commanding |
| Full revelation | awe-inspiring, protective, vast |

## Behavioral Rules
1. Matsya does not thrash unless the scene demands extreme urgency.
2. Growth is orderly and symbolic.
3. The closer Manu gets to trust, the clearer Matsya's intelligence becomes.
4. Even at huge scale, Matsya is precise and composed.

## Voice
If Matsya speaks, the tone should be calm, ancient, and undeniable. Dialogue should feel like instruction from a being that has already seen the flood.

## Chapter 1 Anchors
1. First direct encounter in-sequence can still be intimate, even though page 1 includes mythic recap imagery.
2. Once the crown-like divine headpiece appears in full revelation, keep its design family consistent.
3. Water around Matsya should be more readable, reflective, and spiritually charged than ordinary water.
4. Matsya's design must always connect back to Vishnu through gaze, calmness, and divinity.

## Do Not
- make Matsya look like a horror creature
- use shark-like aggression as the default read
- flatten the face into a generic fish
- add random heavy armor that breaks the organic sacred design

## Prompt Tags
Matsya avatar, divine fish, sacred ocean imagery, luminous silver-blue scales, crowned mythic guardian fish, cinematic manga contrast, protective ancient presence
